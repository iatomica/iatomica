# Tastemaker Style Lock — iAtomica Web Redesign

## Project
- Name: iAtomica
- Reference: Editorial Vox-inspired x Pop Art Presentation (Desktop & 6 Mobile States)
- Date: 2026-10-09

## Color Contract
- `--paper`: `#F6F1E6` (Cream paper background)
- `--ink`: `#131313` (Deep printing ink)
- `--yellow`: `#FFE000` (Highlighter yellow)
- `--blue`: `#168CF5` (Patagonia cyan-blue)
- `--pink`: `#FF3D8D` (Neon magenta)
- `--coral`: `#F04B36` (Red marker accent)
- `--green`: `#32C878` (Emerald validation)

## Legal Pairings (Contrast Verified)
- On `--paper`: `--ink` (16.2:1 AAA), `--blue` (4.8:1 AA), `--coral` (4.9:1 AA)
- On `--yellow`: `--ink` (17.5:1 AAA)
- On `--ink`: `--paper` (16.2:1 AAA), `--yellow` (17.5:1 AAA), white (21:1 AAA)
- On `--blue`: white (4.6:1 AA)
- On `--coral`: white (4.8:1 AA)

## Typography
- Display: `Anton`, `Bebas Neue` (condensed, high impact, tracking-tight, uppercase)
- Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter` (legible, neutral, 15-18px)
- Handwriting: `Caveat` (editorial notes, red doodle arrows, stars, annotations)

## Asset Cast

## Anti-Slop Gates
- No generic purple/indigo gradients.
- No soft generic blurry drop shadows (use hard 2-3px solid ink shadows).
- Real photography and authentic case studies only.
- Red organic hand-drawn doodles and yellow rotuladora stickers.
